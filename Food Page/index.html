Simple Omelette Receipe

This easy, fail-proof omelette recipe is truly the best. With a variety of filling suggestions, you will learn how to make an omelet at home that’s even more delicious than your favorite breakfast restaurant (but so simple)

Preparation Time

  • Total: Approximately 10 minutes
  • Preparation: 5 minutes
  • Cooking: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2–3 large eggs
  • Salt, to taste
  • Pepper, to taste
  • 1 tablespoon of butter or oil
  • Optional fillings: cheese, diced vegetables, cooked meats, herbs

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs: Mix eggs with a pinch of salt and pepper until well combined. (Optional: Add a tablespoon of water or milk for fluffiness.)
  2. Heat the pan: Place a non-stick pan on medium heat and add butter or oil.
  3. Cook the omelette: Pour eggs into the pan once the butter is bubbling. Tilt to spread evenly.
  4. Add fillings (optional): When eggs start setting but are still slightly runny, add fillings to one half.
  5. Fold and serve: Fold the omelette in half over the fillings, cook for a minute, then slide onto a plate.
  6. Enjoy: Serve hot, seasoning more if needed.
